How it works

There is one invitation. The link carries the functions, so the guest sees only their own cards — and the dates, the countdown and the footer follow whatever those cards are.

Nothing is stored anywhere. The link is the configuration, so inviting someone new never needs a code change or a redeploy.

The plain site address, with no ?e= on the end, shows every function. That is the general invitation — use it for anyone invited to everything.
